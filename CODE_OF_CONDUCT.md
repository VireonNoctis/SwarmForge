# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in this project is expected to help keep the community welcoming, respectful, and constructive.

Examples of behavior that supports this standard include:

- Being considerate and respectful of differing viewpoints.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Respecting privacy and avoiding harassment or personal attacks.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discrimination, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports should be sent privately through the reporting channel documented in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action when conduct is harmful or disruptive.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
